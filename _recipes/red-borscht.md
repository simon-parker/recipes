## Ingredients

- 1/2 head cabbage
- 1 onion
- 1 good beet root; buy 2 as backup
- 1 1/2 red peppers
- 1 1/2 containers frozen baby lima beans
- 1 large carrot, or 2–3 smaller carrots
- 1/2 container Sclafani crushed tomatoes
- Beef soup flavor cubes
- Sunflower oil
- Salt and sugar
- 1 celery root, optional
- 1 jalapeño pepper, optional

## Recipe

1. Add chopped red peppers, thinly sliced cabbage, lima beans, sliced jalapeño, and enough water to almost cover to a pot. Leave room for the remaining ingredients.
2. Cover and set over high heat.
3. In a skillet over medium heat, add 2 tablespoons sunflower oil, sliced onion, thinly sliced carrot, and sliced celery root. Stir occasionally.
4. Peel beet and discard imperfect or light areas.
5. When the pot is ready to boil, add beef soup flavor cubes.
6. Add crushed tomatoes to the skillet.
7. Grate the beet.
8. When the pot boils, reduce to low.
9. When orange-red fat appears in the skillet, add skillet contents to the pot.
10. Wash the skillet, add 1 teaspoon oil, and return to medium heat.
11. Add grated beet and 1 teaspoon sugar to the skillet.
12. Once beet is soft, add juice from less than 1/2 lemon or lime.
13. Salt the pot to taste and keep it at a low boil.
14. Add beets to the pot. Rinse remaining oil and beets from the skillet with broth and add to the pot.
15. Cover the pot and turn off heat; it will continue cooking.
16. Add remaining beet before refrigerating several hours later to improve color.
