## Ingredients

- 300 g frozen white mushrooms
- 2 containers baby bella mushrooms
- 2 containers lima beans
- 1 large carrot
- 1 large onion
- 1 mushroom flavor cube, Telma
- Olive oil
- Salt
- Parsley and dill

## Recipe

1. Rinse white mushrooms thoroughly and add to a pot.
2. Fill pot a little more than halfway with water.
3. Bring to a boil over high heat, then reduce to low. Skim foam whenever it forms.
4. Wash baby bella mushrooms, trim stems and imperfections, slice, wash again, and add to the pot.
5. Dice onion. Peel carrot and cut into small pieces.
6. Add crushed mushroom flavor cube to the pot.
7. In a medium-large pan over medium heat, add olive oil and onion. Stir occasionally.
8. Wash lima beans and add them to the pot.
9. Add carrots to the pan a few minutes after the onions. Continue stirring occasionally.
10. Salt the soup as needed.
11. Once carrot is soft and bright, add pan contents to the soup. Rinse the pan with water or stock and add that to the pot.
12. Simmer on low 15–20 minutes.
13. Turn off the heat and add parsley or dill.
14. Let sit for a few hours, then refrigerate.
