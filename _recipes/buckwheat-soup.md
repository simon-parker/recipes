## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup roasted buckwheat groats
- 2 containers frozen lima beans
- 1 medium onion
- 1 medium carrot
- Vegetable flavor cubes, or vegetable stock
- 1 bunch parsley
- 1 bunch dill

## Recipe

1. Fill a pot with water, or vegetable stock, and set over high heat.
2. Cook buckwheat until almost done.
3. When the pot is near boiling, add lima beans and cooked buckwheat.
4. Sauté onions and carrots.
5. Once the pot boils, add the sautéed vegetables and flavor cubes, if using.
6. Cover, reduce heat to low, and cook 15–20 minutes.
7. Turn off the heat and add parsley and dill.
