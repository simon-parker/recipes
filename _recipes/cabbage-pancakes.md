## Ingredients

- Cabbage, thinly sliced
- Salt
- 1–2 eggs
- Pepper
- Flour, enough to thicken

## Recipe

1. Thinly slice cabbage.
2. Add salt and mix.
3. Add eggs and pepper; mix.
4. Add flour until thick enough.
5. Fry as pancakes.
