## Ingredients

Makes one loaf, about 980 g baked weight.

### Sourdough build

- 22 g active rye starter
- 66 g medium rye flour
- 66 g water

### Sponge

- All sourdough build
- 150 g medium rye flour
- 80 g water

### Final dough

- All sponge
- 152 g medium rye flour
- 250 g sifted whole-grain spelt flour, or first-grade wheat flour
- 290 g water, plus 10–15 g if needed
- 10 g salt

## Recipe

1. Mix the sourdough build. Cover and ferment 9 hours at 75°F, or 12 hours at 72°F, until 1.5–2x risen.
2. Mix the sponge. Cover and ferment 4 hours at 86°F, until 1.5–2x risen.
3. Mix the final dough until uniform. It should be wet and sticky. Cover and ferment 2–2.5 hours at 86°F.
4. Transfer to a greased loaf pan and smooth/shape with wet hands or a wet spatula.
5. Proof 60 minutes at 77°F.
6. Bake on a preheated stone: 482°F for 10 minutes, 464°F for 20 minutes, then 356°F for 20 minutes.
7. Cool uncovered on a rack for 2–3 hours. Best sliced after 24 hours.

## Notes

Total flour is 380 g rye and 250 g spelt/wheat: about 60% rye.
