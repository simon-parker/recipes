## Ingredients

- 1 bunch spinach
- 2 medium long potatoes
- Parsley and dill, a handful
- 1 medium carrot
- 2 small onions, or 1 medium onion
- 1 lemon
- 3 eggs
- Olive oil
- Salt
- 2 flavor cubes

## Recipe

1. Hard-boil the eggs. Cool in cold water, peel, and chop finely.
2. Remove spinach stems and wash thoroughly.
3. Fill a soup pot halfway with water. Add cut potatoes and set over medium heat.
4. Chop parsley and dill finely.
5. Chop onions finely. Peel and finely slice carrot.
6. Warm a pan, add 1 tablespoon olive oil and onions. When transparent, add carrots and sauté until the carrots release color.
7. When potatoes boil, skim foam, add the pan contents, and lower the heat.
8. Add flavor cubes.
9. After a few minutes, add lemon juice, eggs, spinach, parsley, and dill.
10. Turn off the heat.
