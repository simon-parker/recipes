## Ingredients

- 2/3 head cauliflower
- 1 1/2 bags frozen green peas
- 1 zucchini
- 2 bell peppers, any color
- 2 medium carrots
- 1 onion
- Parsley and dill
- 1 chicken flavor cube
- Olive oil
- Salt

## Recipe

1. Fill a large bowl with water to collect vegetables.
2. Discard cauliflower leaves, cut cauliflower into medium pieces, and add to the bowl.
3. Peel and cut zucchini into medium pieces; add to the bowl.
4. Cut bell peppers into small pieces; add to the bowl.
5. Finely chop parsley and dill; set aside.
6. Finely chop onion. Slice carrots thinly.
7. Add a spoon of olive oil to a skillet over medium heat.
8. Add onion. Once transparent, add carrots. Stir occasionally.
9. Add water and the bowl contents to a pot over medium-high heat.
10. Add crushed chicken flavor cube.
11. When the pot begins to boil, the carrots should be cooked through and orange color should have spread.
12. Add skillet contents to the pot, including the oil. Reduce heat to low.
13. When cauliflower is ready, about 20 minutes, add frozen peas.
14. Salt to taste.
15. Add parsley and dill, then turn off the heat.
